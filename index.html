    Auto-fill by name (optional)

    Type a title and the app fills in the poster, type and plot from TMDB. It needs your own free key: create an account at themoviedb.org, open Settings, then API, request a key, and paste it here. The key stays on this phone only.

    Backup

    Your list is saved only in this browser, on this device. Clearing your browser data erases it, so save a backup file now and then. Your TMDB key is not included in backups.
